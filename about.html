About

Researcher.
Builder. Founder.

From training NLP models in a lab to co-founding an AI company - here's the path, and what drives it.

I'm a machine learning engineer and researcher with 5+ years building AI systems - specializing in NLP and large language models. Today I'm Co-Founder & Head of AI at Purands, building agents that plan and act on their own.

From training NLP models as an intern to leading AI at a company I co-founded, the throughline has stayed the same: build systems that don't just predict - they decide, and act. My north star is to make AI that meaningfully amplifies what people can do, steadily toward Artificial General Intelligence.

How I work

Principles I build by

01

A benchmark isn't a win

A model only matters when it holds up in production, in front of real users. That's the bar I build to - not the leaderboard.

02

Working beats perfect

The shortest path to a great system is a working one shipped early, then sharpened every week with what users actually do.

03

Build for the decade

Every project is a step toward autonomous, genuinely useful AI. I'd rather make the move that compounds than the one that demos.
